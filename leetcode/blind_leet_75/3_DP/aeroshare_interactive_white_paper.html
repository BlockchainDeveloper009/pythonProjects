The Problem with Centralized Platforms

Today's digital content landscape is controlled by gatekeepers, creating risks and inequities for users and creators.

⛓️

Centralization & Censorship

A single point of failure means content can be removed or censored at any time, without community consent.

🛡️

Security & Privacy Risks

Centralized servers are prime targets for data breaches, compromising the privacy and security of millions of users.

💸

Inequitable Value

The economic value generated by the community is captured by platform owners, not the users who create and curate the content.

The AeroShare Solution

A decentralized application (dApp) that puts power back in the hands of the community.

Decentralized P2P Storage

Users share books directly from their devices. A distributed hash table (DHT) helps locate content across the network, making it resilient and censorship-resistant. Book metadata is secured on the blockchain.

Community-Driven Verification

A network of user "librarians" certifies books as safe and high-quality, earning reputation and token rewards. This builds a trusted, community-curated library without central moderators.

In-App Marketplace & DAO

Users can share for free or charge a small fee in AERO tokens. The entire ecosystem is governed by the AeroShare DAO, where token holders vote on the platform's future.

Phase 1: MVP Launch

Launch a closed beta with core P2P sharing functionality and a basic reputation system. Gather early user feedback to refine the core experience and prepare for a public launch.

Phase 2: Token Launch

Introduce the AERO token and implement the initial reward distribution model for sharers and verifiers. Launch a public version of the app on iOS and Android.

Phase 3: DAO Activation

Transition to full DAO governance. The community will gain control over the platform's treasury and development roadmap through on-chain voting.

Phase 4: Ecosystem Expansion

Expand beyond books to include other digital content types, such as audiobooks, academic papers, and independent comics. Explore cross-chain interoperability and Layer 2 scaling solutions.

Technical Architecture

A robust, scalable, and decentralized stack.

Frontend Layer

A cross-platform mobile app (iOS & Android) built with a modern framework for a fluid, native user experience.

Peer-to-Peer Layer

A custom P2P protocol using a Distributed Hash Table (DHT) for efficient content discovery and end-to-end encrypted file transfers.

Blockchain Layer

A scalable, low-cost blockchain for the immutable ledger of metadata and token transactions, with smart contracts automating rewards and governance.
